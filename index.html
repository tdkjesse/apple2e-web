APPLE IIe WEB — Phase 7

(1×–8×)

Floppy drives (.dsk 140 KB or .nib 232960 bytes)

Experimental Apple IIe bus and keyboard. 16 KiB ROM loading is supported, but full ROM boot compatibility, graphics, slot ROM and bootable Disk II emulation are not yet implemented. Experimental slot 6 boot attempts require both local ROM files and a mounted disk. The read latch is approximate and not cycle-accurate, so a boot attempt may fail or hang. ROM execution is experimental; unsupported opcodes are reported in status.
